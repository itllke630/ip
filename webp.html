About WebP & Image Format Conversion

What is WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides both lossy and lossless compression. Compared to JPG, WebP files are typically 25–35% smaller at the same visual quality. Compared to PNG, WebP lossless images are 26% smaller on average. It also supports transparency (alpha channel) and animation — making it a versatile replacement for JPG, PNG, and GIF in a single format.

Why Convert Between Formats?

Different use cases call for different formats. WebP is ideal for websites (faster loading, less bandwidth), but some older applications and operating systems don't support it. PNG is best for screenshots, logos, and images requiring lossless quality with transparency. JPG remains the most universally compatible format, perfect for photos and sharing across all devices and platforms.

WebP → PNG

Convert WebP to PNG when you need lossless quality or when working with applications that don't yet support WebP (older image editors, some CMS platforms, legacy email clients). PNG preserves every pixel perfectly and supports full transparency.

WebP / PNG → JPG

Convert to JPG for maximum compatibility and smaller file sizes for photographs. Use the quality slider to balance between file size and visual fidelity. JPG is universally supported across every device, browser, and platform — ideal for sharing and uploading.

PNG / JPG → WebP

Convert to WebP to optimize for the web. Dramatically reduce image file sizes without noticeable quality loss — faster page loads, lower bandwidth consumption, and improved SEO rankings. Most modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) fully support WebP.
